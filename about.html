Who We Are

Technology That
Serves Real People

Camerin Innovate is a subsidiary of the Camerin Group, based at Carnival Infopark Phase 1, Kakkanad, Kochi. We specialize in healthcare information technology solutions and enterprise software for growing businesses.

Our flagship product, LIFE HIS, is a patient-centric, fully integrated healthcare delivery platform built to help hospitals and clinics manage clinical operations, administrative workflows, billing, and patient records — all in one place.

Beyond healthcare, we develop custom software for HR, CRM, digital operations, and brand development — built around the specific needs of each organization we work with, not off-the-shelf templates.

Our approach is simple: understand the problem first, then build the right solution. We don't sell software. We build long-term partnerships.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

These aren't poster words. They are the principles that guide every decision we make — in design, development, and client relationships.

Trust First
We build trust before we build anything else. Every commitment we make to a client is a commitment we keep.
Problem-First Thinking
We don't start with a product and look for customers. We start with a real problem and build the exact solution it needs.
People Over Process
Technology should serve the people using it — not the other way around. Every system we build is tested against real human workflows.
Measurable Impact
We measure success by outcomes — reduced wait times, faster billing, fewer errors. Not by features shipped.
Continuous Learning
Healthcare and technology both move fast. Our team stays ahead — not to follow trends, but to serve our clients better.
Responsible Growth
We grow by making our clients successful. Their growth is the only metric that matters to us in the long term.
A History To Be Proud Of

Our Journey Since 2015

You don't have to be great to start, but you have to start to be great.

2015
The Beginning
Camerin began its journey with a passion for making people's lives easier. CamerinFolks launched its first product, CF Stock — a retail management tool that quickly gained acceptance in both the UAE and Indian markets.
2016
Entering Healthcare
The company expanded into healthcare and logistics, releasing several products capable of automating operations from small clinics to larger hospital chains. A dedicated web design team was formed to deliver the best UI experience to end users.
2018
LIFE HIS Platform
Camerin Innovate launched LIFE HIS — a patient-centric, fully integrated healthcare delivery platform built to enable hospitals to deliver services using best-in-class administrative and clinical practices.
Now
200+ Strong
Today Camerin Innovate operates from Carnival Infopark, Kochi with 200+ team members, 70+ clients, and 50+ products — serving hospitals and enterprises across India and internationally.
Our Objective

To be globally known as a firm that conducts business responsibly and ethically — thriving in healthcare, education, and corporate training. Growing international sales annually while forming the foundation for sound decision-making.
