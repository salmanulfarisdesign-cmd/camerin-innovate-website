Common Questions

Before You Reach Out, Some Answers

How long does a typical implementation take?
It depends on the product and the size of your organization. A typical LIFE HIS implementation for a mid-size hospital takes 4–8 weeks including setup, data migration, and staff training. Simpler products like KOT or HR systems can be live in 1–2 weeks.
Do you provide training and post-launch support?
Yes. Every implementation includes hands-on staff training before go-live, and we provide ongoing support after launch. We consider a project complete only when your team is fully confident using the system.
Is LIFE HIS NABH compliant?
Yes. LIFE HIS is built with NABH (National Accreditation Board for Hospitals) compliance standards in mind. The system supports the documentation, audit trails, and reporting requirements that NABH accreditation demands.
Can the software be customized for our specific workflows?
Customization is central to how we work. We don't sell off-the-shelf boxes. Every implementation goes through a needs analysis phase where we map your actual workflows and configure or build the system around them.
What does a free consultation include?
A 30–45 minute call with one of our consultants. We'll listen to your current challenges, ask questions about your operations, and give you an honest assessment of what solution would help — including whether ours is the right fit.
